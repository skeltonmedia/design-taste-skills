# Interview question bank

Use the core loop in `interview/SKILL.md` first. Reach for these when an answer stalls at "I like it" or "it feels off". One question at a time. Never supply the adjective.

## Opening a session

- "Ranked gut call, ten seconds each, before we talk."
- "Which one would you be embarrassed to have shipped?" (ask at the end too; the answer sometimes changes)

## When the eye is the clue

- "What did you notice first?"
- "Where did your eye go second?"
- "What did you not look at?"

## Pushing to the specific

- "What specifically is doing that?"
- "If I covered the type, would it still do that? If I covered the color?"
- "Is it something that is there, or something that is missing?"
- "Point at it."

## The transfer test

- "If we stole only that one move for your work, what would it touch?"
- "Would it survive on a phone? On paper? In your product's worst screen?"
- "Is that the piece, or is that the move?"

## The trade

- "What did this design give up to get that?"
- "Who would hate this, and would you mind?"
- "What would it cost you to do this: a tool, a skill, a person?" (if the cost is unknown, mark `[aspiration]`)

## When they reject something

- "What is the first thing you would delete?"
- "Is it wrong, or is it not yours?" (both are data; only the second is taste)
- "Have you rejected something like this before? When?"
- "What would it take for this to pass?"

## Synthesis

- "What do your top picks share that your bottom pick lacks?"
- "Say the rule in one sentence, as if telling a new hire."
- "What would break if we followed that rule everywhere?" (the answer seeds Known tensions)

## Client additions

- "Show me the competitor that makes you angry."
- "What have you rejected before: an agency comp, a rebrand, a template? Do you still have it?"
- "What do customers say your product looks like? Do you agree?"

## Do not ask

- "What do you think?" (invites a review, not a reaction)
- "Do you like it?" (mood, not signal)
- "Would you call this minimal / warm / bold?" (your word, not theirs)
